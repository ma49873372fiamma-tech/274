Privacy Overview

SALVUS X is a utility application designed for privacy-conscious users. We are committed to providing tools that operate exclusively on your device without collecting, transmitting, or storing personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your privacy.

Data Collection & Storage

No Data Collection: SALVUS X does not collect, monitor, or track any personal information. We do not access your contacts, location, or device identifiers for analytical purposes.

  • Local Only: All calculations and interactions are performed locally on your Android device. No data leaves your phone.
  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account to use this application. No login credentials are stored or transmitted.
  • No Advertising: The application does not display third-party advertisements and does not track your behavior for marketing purposes.

Permissions & Usage

SALVUS X may request minimal system permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality (e.g., storage access for saving calculation history locally if enabled). These permissions are never used for data extraction or transmission. We do not request permissions for access to microphones, cameras, or precise location.

Third-Party Services

This application does not embed any third-party trackers, analytics software, or advertising SDKs that transmit user data to external servers. The software runs entirely in isolation from external data collection services.

Security

Because no personal data is collected or stored on external servers, there is no risk of data breaches related to user information. Any local data you choose to save (such as calculator history) remains encrypted within your device’s secure storage and can be wiped at any time by clearing the app data.

Children’s Privacy

The application is not directed at children under the age of 13. Given that the app does not collect any user data, it does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date or other means we deem appropriate.
